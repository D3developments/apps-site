Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Personal Backup is a single-user utility. Its only user is its owner.

What Google data it accesses

It uses Google's drive.file permission, which allows access only to files the utility itself creates in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file.

How it uses that data

It uses that access to copy the owner's own backup files to the owner's Google Drive and to restore them. It does nothing else with Google data.

Where data is stored

Data is stored only in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own server.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with, sold to, or used by anyone else. No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Removing access

The owner can delete the files in Google Drive at any time, and can remove the utility's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
